# 0016. Postures with tested collision shapes; a static four-point gait

Status: accepted, 2026-11-23 (Lesson 16).

## Context

Phase 4 calls for a body that crawls through low gaps and scrambles up slabs too steep to walk. Lessons 00–15 give the player one collision shape, the upright capsule of Lesson 00 (radius 0.35 m, height 1.8 m), and one walkable limit, `floor_max_angle` 45° (Lesson 02). Lesson 14 crouches the drawn body without changing the capsule; Lesson 15 makes the capsule kinematic while climbing. Lessons 12–13 supply raycast contacts, two-bone IK, the center of mass and the support polygon. Three questions needed an answer: how the collision shape follows the posture without being placed inside geometry, how four limbs are moved so that the body stays over its contacts, and what lets the body move up a slab that the walking rule calls a wall.

## Decision

1. **Four postures, one state machine, every transition guarded.** `PostureController` (a `StanceController` subclass, so the Lesson 14 shift and crouch keep working) holds STAND, CROUCH, CRAWL and SCRAMBLE. Every request tests the new posture's collision shape at the player's position with `PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D.intersect_shape()` before the shape is swapped; a refused request leaves the posture and gives a reason ("no headroom" for a taller shape, "no room" for a lower but longer one). The shape is tested 0.02 m above the floor, so that the floor itself does not count as an obstacle.
2. **Three capsules, each enclosing the drawn body.** STAND is the scene's capsule. CROUCH is an upright capsule 1.55 m tall (the crouched head reaches 1.52 m while walking). CRAWL is a capsule of the standing radius lying along the facing, 1.65 m long, its center 0.225 m behind the origin (the drawn crawling body is 0.69 m high and reaches from 1.02 m behind the origin to 0.57 m ahead). SCRAMBLE uses the CROUCH capsule. The shapes are built in code from exports; the scene's capsule is swapped, never edited.
3. **The crawl turns only where the turned shape fits.** A lying capsule sweeps sideways when it turns; each turn step is tested with the same shape query.
4. **Scrambling raises the walkable limit.** In SCRAMBLE, `floor_max_angle` is 55°; the slab (48°) becomes floor to the capsule. The transition is automatic: a ray 0.6 m ahead along the stick finds ground that rises or falls more than 0.05 m with a slope between the walking and the scrambling limits, and the stick pushes toward it. Scrambling ends when the ground under the body and ahead of it is less steep than 40°.
5. **A static gait on four contacts.** `CrawlController` moves hands and knees (crawl) or hands and feet (scramble) in the lateral sequence (left leg, left hand, right leg, right hand), each limb in the air for 0.2 of the cycle, so at most one limb is lifted. The cycle advances with the distance moved, as Lesson 11's walk phase does. Before a limb lifts, the ground point of the center of mass (Lesson 13) must lie inside the triangle of the other three contacts (signed distance ≥ 0); otherwise the limb waits and the body stops, while a sideways weight shift (a `CriticalSpring`, Lesson 14) moves the body toward the next triangle. After 1 s of waiting the limb lifts anyway, and the lift is counted as unstable. On a slope the body leans up the slope by half the drift d tan θ of the center of mass's vertical projection.
6. **The body is posed in world space, each limb alone.** The pelvis and the shoulders lie on a line parallel to the plane of the contacts; arms and scrambling legs use `TwoBoneIk` (Lesson 12); a crawling leg aims the thigh at the knee's contact and lays the shin back along the ground. A posture change blends the bone poses over 0.4 s.
7. **The balance monitor reads the crawl's contacts.** `CrawlBalanceMonitor` (a `ClimbBalanceMonitor` subclass) builds the support polygon from the planted limbs while the body crawls or scrambles; the Lesson 13 OSC messages carry the new margin unchanged. `PostureController` sends `/body/posture s` on every change.

## Consequences

- No Lesson 00–15 source or scene file changes. The Lesson 16 scene swaps the player scene and the scripts of `Stance` and `BalanceMonitor`, and adds `Crawl`, `CrawlGap` and `Slab`. The `run/main_scene` check moved from `test_15_climb_scene.gd` to `test_16_crawl_scene.gd`; the input-map assertions of Lesson 15 are kept there and repeated in the Lesson 16 test.
- A horizontal lying capsule fits only on almost level ground. With the upright capsule's origin floating r (1/cos θ − 1) above a slope, the fit test accepts the crawl while tan θ ≤ 0.02 / e, e the distance of the axis's uphill end from the origin: 4.6° facing up, 1.6° facing down (measured: fits at 4.75° and not at 4.80°, fits at 1.66° and not at 1.68°). Across a slope it fits at every angle, because the lying radius is no larger than the standing radius. Lesson 16 places its gap on a level floor.
- The scramble's upright capsule does not follow the drawn body along the slab: hands and feet reach about 0.6 m up and down the slope from the capsule's axis. Nothing near the slab meets them; an overhang above a slab would.
- The shape changes at once and the drawn body follows: after a request to crouch the head is above the new shape for 0.22 s, after a request to crawl for 0.38 s.
- The gait is static and slow: about 0.37 m/s on the level with the defaults (measured under the roof), of which a fifth of the time is spent waiting for the balance. It has no dynamics: no momentum, no slip of a contact.
- Turning on the spot advances the gait by the arc of the turn; in the passage the planted limbs stay within 0.02 m of their contacts through a turn and the turn back. A crawl that reverses its direction does not: a limb planted while the body backs up lies behind its home point when the body moves forward, and the limb error reaches 0.09–0.20 m until its next swing (measured; Lesson 16 E9).
- `PostureController` refuses every request while `ClimbController` climbs. Key 4 then toggles the Lesson 14 stance only, which the climb reads (Lesson 15), and the posture follows the stance through a tested request after the climb.
