This project will be used as a way for me to work on my technical skills in the realm of game development.

I am a professional level musician and I have strong audio skills, particularly in Ableton Live and SuperCollider programming.  I have less experience with game audio.

I have no 3D modeling experience but have strong math and geoemtry skills so I want to get into procedural math based rendering and modeling, possibly using SDFs.  I am an OK visual artist and can use Procreate to draw.

My programming skill is intermediate.

My main artistic goal is to create a sense of place and space that a character can traverse with a tactile feeling.  I am inspired by the walking simulator aspect of death stranding, particularly the accurate normal maps on the terrain and the way the character realistically traverses over complex geometry.  The delivery aspect is not my goal; I want to just make cool geometry that I can feel as a player through traversal.  I would love to also include crawling/climbing/scrambling, as if travelling up rocky formations in a place like Joshua Tree.

I will rely on AI to fill in gaps but I want to learn the technical skills along the way.  Always keep that in mind -- turn new material into tutorials and challenges for me to tweak and solve, as if training a junior game developer.  Be as socratic and step-by-step as possible.

I am open to using any engine.  Godot or Unity would be my first choices but I am open to whatever is best, including writing a simple purpose-built engine from scratch.

I will handle version control with Git.

I want to make diagetic audio using physical simulation and synthesis to create natural sounds, both of the ambience and the player interacting with the environment.  I can also create samples.  I will create music too but would love the music to be partly generative, either using SuperCollider via OSC or another standard game audio tool so that the player's movements and environment influence the audio as well as the composition.

I want some basic textbox/dialog systems too, though that will not be the focus.  Same goes for UI.

Set up an environment and some basic ideas to work with.  The eventual goals are:
- a natural environment with rocks, vegetation, flowing water, various textures of earth, topography, all geometrically normal mapped to interact with the player.
- as much as possible I want to use (differential) geometry (see the DiscreteDifferentialGeometry pdf) to create surfaces and effects, rather than having to model by hand.  Though I am excited to learn about modeling, later on, with help.
- a humanoid player that can walk, crawl, climb, and balance, with sensitive analog control, even more sensitive than the triggers in Death Stranding, the main focus of the player being balancing in various stances
- beautiful lighting that remains performant.  Realism is not the goal but I want to play with various lighting methods, equations, pipelines, and tradeoffs to get something striking
- music and audio that is primarily diagetic and interactive/generative, including natural sounds
- a robust editing interface that separates code and assets and lets me swap out assets as I create them.
- walkthroughs of the technical (coding) that this project requires, to strengthen my skills as someone getting deeper into game development.
